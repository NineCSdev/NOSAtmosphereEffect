Atmo Engine 7.0.2 introduces separate Google Play and F-Droid builds. The Play build restores higher-quality ML Kit subject segmentation with an explicit model download, while the F-Droid build bundles the open-source U2NetP model for fully offline use. Both retain the new Material 3 interface, real effect previews, theme playlists, and device palette fixes, and now target Android API 36.
